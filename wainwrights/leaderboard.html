Leaderboard — Terms & Conditions

By joining the leaderboard, you agree to the following:

  • Your chosen display name and your Wainwright progress (total fells climbed, completion percentage, and total elevation climbed) will be visible to anyone else signed in to this tracker.
  • The specific fells you've climbed are not shared — only these totals.
  • This is a personal, non-commercial feature built for friends and family using this tracker. It isn't a registered service and comes with no guarantees about uptime, accuracy, or data retention.
  • You can leave the leaderboard at any time with the "Leave leaderboard" button, which removes your display name and stops your progress being shown to others.
  • Please don't use a display name that could identify someone else, or anything offensive — entries may be removed at the discretion of whoever runs this tracker.
  • If you add an Instagram handle, it will show as a public, tappable link to that profile next to your name. Make sure it's genuinely your own account — don't add someone else's handle.
  • Data is stored via Supabase. Use the usual sensible judgement — don't put anything in your display name beyond what you're happy for others using this tracker to see.
